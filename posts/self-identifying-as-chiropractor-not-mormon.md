> Other jobs might make demands on your skills, but if you are deficient you can do something about it. A lumberjack or a musician can improve with practice: try harder. But lumberjacks and musicians are not required to believe.  
> 
> <cite>– @dawkinsBooksFurnishLife2021</cite>

This is as good an explanation as any other for why I continue to identify as a chiropractor but not a Mormon. 

To be a Mormon, [you must believe](https://youtu.be/GVJgmp2Tc2s?si=aFfmq5az9i06tS7V). 

To be a chiropractor does not require belief... al least, not anymore.
